Privacy policy

Your dungeon stays with you.

Last updated: 2026-10-20
Developer: Alex Olson
Privacy contact: aolson078@gmail.com

EmberDraw is an offline Android card game. This policy explains how the game handles information and how to contact its developer.

Information used on your device

The game stores your current dungeon, dungeon seed, run history and statistics, achievements, unlocked content, earned Embers and upgrades in its private app storage. It also stores settings such as language, sound, text size, contrast, motion and tutorial hints. This information is used to run the game, resume your progress and apply your preferences. The game may use your device's language setting to select its interface language.

The game does not require an EmberDraw account. It does not send your game progress or settings to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, analytics or third-party automatic crash-reporting software, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or personal files.

If you choose Copy seed, the game places that dungeon seed on Android's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. Any app or service you choose to paste it into handles the copied information under its own practices. Clipboard handling and synchronization can also depend on your device settings.

Storage, retention and removal

Progress and preferences are stored locally while you use the game. The game retains up to 100 recent run results; newer results replace older entries. It has no developer-operated cloud save or account recovery service.

You can remove locally stored game data through Android's app settings by clearing EmberDraw's storage. This permanently removes your local progress and preferences. Uninstalling normally removes the app's private data as well. Android or your device manufacturer may separately retain or transfer data according to your device settings and system behavior. We cannot recover a lost local save from a developer server.

The app requests that Android cloud backup be disabled. Some device manufacturers may still support device-to-device transfer. EmberDraw does not guarantee transfer, restore, or synchronization between devices or between the separate Test app and the production app.

Purchases and platform services

Google Play handles purchasing, downloading and updating the paid app under Google's terms and privacy practices. EmberDraw does not request your payment-card information and has no in-app purchase processing. Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may separately process store, account, security or diagnostic information according to their own settings and policies. Those services are distinct from the game's local progress storage.

Contacting us

If you contact Alex Olson at aolson078@gmail.com, we receive the contact details and information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information; crop screenshots to show only the problem.

Support email is handled by Alex Olson using Google Gmail. Google processes email as the service provider under its own privacy practices. Support information is used to respond to requests and investigate problems; it is not sold or used for advertising. Information may be disclosed when required by law.

Ordinary support emails are deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved, except records that must be retained for legal or accounting obligations. To request deletion of information you sent to support, email aolson078@gmail.com, preferably from the address used for the original request. This does not delete a game save that exists only on your phone. Provider-managed copies and backups follow Google's retention practices.

Security

The game uses Android's private app storage for progress and preferences. No storage system provides an absolute security guarantee. Protect your phone and be cautious about granting debugging access or sharing backups and screenshots containing information you want to keep private.

Children and audience

EmberDraw is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not request a birth date or create player accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to support, contact aolson078@gmail.com so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate, subject to applicable obligations. The intended audience does not assign a store content rating.

Hosted policy page

This public copy of the policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that page sends a normal web request to GitHub, which may process technical request information, including the visitor's IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The policy page includes no developer-added analytics, advertising, contact form, remote fonts or tracking scripts. Reading the copy inside the game does not open the hosted page or send a web request.

Policy updates and questions

We may revise this policy when the app's data practices change. The policy and its last-updated date are available inside the app. Contact aolson078@gmail.com with questions about this policy or information you have sent to support.
